Google Ads feedback

Three Sisters Hotel & Tallinn City Apartments · Mathias Randruut · 5 October 2026

Short version

  1. Keywords are fine. Leave them.
  2. Conversion tracking is missing. That is the biggest gap. There is no data to optimize on.
  3. Ad text is factual. It should sell the experience, with social proof.
  4. Tallinn City Apartments overpays for brand terms. Cap bids at about €1.50.

1Set up conversion tracking first

Right now the Three Sisters account has no conversion data. Web purchases on threesistershotel.ee are not tracked, so there is nothing to optimize against.

What I would do

  • Track the CTA buttons – the clicks that start a room booking.
  • Feed those clicks into Google Ads as conversions.
  • Switch the bid strategy to Maximize conversions.
Why this comes before everything else

Phrase match is an in-between match type. It does not use Google's machine learning to the max. Once conversions are flowing in, we can broaden the keywords and let the system find bookers we are not reaching today. Without conversion data, broadening just spends more.

Tracking is the one change that unlocks the others.

2Ad text

The current ads say the facts well: Old Town location, the history, the three merchant houses, boutique positioning, the direct-booking offer. But they read like a description of a hotel, not an invitation.

Three Sisters is not simply a place to sleep in Tallinn. The appeal is the feeling of stepping into the romantic Old Town, where medieval history and boutique luxury come together. The copy should make people imagine what it feels like to stay here.

What I would improve

  • Social proof. Show that other people value and remember this stay.
  • Stronger luxury positioning. "Boutique hotel" does not say refined, intimate, high-end.
  • Sell the Old Town dream. Medieval walls plus modern luxury is stronger than "600 years of history".
  • Experience over hotel. "Hotel" is functional.
  • Romantic, atmospheric language. The property has an old-world dream quality the copy misses.
  • Make the history experiential. Not "dates to 1362" but living inside centuries of Tallinn history.
Suggested headlines
  1. Your Old Town Fairytale
  2. The Old Town Experience
  3. Sleep in a Medieval House
  4. 4.8★ From 1,000+ Guests
  5. Luxury Behind Medieval Walls
  6. More than a Place to Stay
  7. Live the Old Town Dream
  8. Boutique Luxury in Old Town
  9. History Meets Modern Luxury
  10. A Boutique Old Town Escape

Use the real rating and review count in #4.

Suggested descriptions
  1. Live inside 600 years of history, where medieval Tallinn meets modern luxury.
  2. Three medieval merchant houses, beautifully reimagined as an intimate luxury experience.
  3. Twenty-three unique rooms, historic details and a romantic Old Town atmosphere.
  4. Book direct for 20% off 3+ nights, plus room upgrades and flexible check-in.

4Tallinn City Apartments – brand campaign

Volume is low, but we are overpaying for the brand terms. The campaign runs Maximize clicks with no bid limit.

What I would do

  • Set a bid limit of about €1.50.
  • Watch auction insights for a few weeks and adjust.
Why it is safe to bid low here

These people are already searching for the brand. As long as we show up, it is already good. We do not have to be first every time.

I expect clicks to get 30–70% cheaper. The range depends on how hard competitors bid on the name – there is more competition than I first thought.

Order I would do it in

  1. Bid cap on Tallinn City Apartments brand – a five-minute change.
  2. Conversion tracking on the Three Sisters booking buttons.
  3. New ad text.
  4. Switch to Maximize conversions, then broaden keywords once the data is there.
